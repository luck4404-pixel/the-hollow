01 Haunting

The Tenant Upstairs

For three weeks the flat above mine played the same slow footsteps, corner to corner, always at 2:14 AM. The landlord finally admitted the flat has been sealed since 1987. The footsteps stopped the night I said hello.

02 Occult

Song of the Third Well

The village elders never spoke of the third well. My grandmother's rule was simple: if you hear humming from it, you do not look over the edge — because whatever is humming is only doing so to check whether anyone is listening. I heard my own voice hum back.

03 Blood

Do Not Press

There was a red button in the hospital basement labelled DO NOT PRESS. The night guard pressed it. Nothing happened — for six days. On the seventh, every door in the building unlocked at once, and something walked out wearing his badge. We know because it still clocks in. There is also a red button at the bottom of this page.

Her face went white. She said I'd said the same thing the morning before the accident I don't remember being in.

D. — hospice volunteer 03:51 AM

Patients near the end sometimes talk to someone standing behind me. You get used to it. What I never got used to was little Mr. Aiyar, three days before he passed, patting the empty chair by my side and saying gently, “Tell her he's been very patient.” That night my mother called. She'd dreamed of my late father. He was waiting, she said, but he wasn't angry. He was just being patient.

M. — night-shift nurse 04:04 AM

Ward 3 has a call bell that rings from an empty bed every third Sunday. Protocol is to cancel it from the desk without going in. One Sunday a new colleague went in out of politeness and said the room was freezing but a woman's voice thanked her for “finally answering.” We transferred her to days. She says on her last night shift, every call bell on the ward rang at once — except that one.
